02Sensors and the 4-20 mA signal

Most field instruments send their reading as a current between 4 and 20 milliamps. Simple, robust over long cables, and it tells you when a wire breaks.

4-20 mA: 4 mA = the bottom of the range, 20 mA = the top. A reheating furnace thermocouple transmitter set for 0 to 1,200 °C sends 4 mA at 0 °C and 20 mA at 1,200 °C. 0 mA means a broken wire, not zero degrees: that is why the scale starts at 4 ("live zero").

The formula the PLC uses: value = (mA − 4) ÷ 16 × range. At 12 mA: (12 − 4) ÷ 16 × 1,200 = 600 °C.

04OPC UA and MQTT: bridging OT and IT

The plant floor (OT) and the office and cloud (IT) speak different languages. OPC UA and MQTT are the two bridges you meet most.

OPC UAA server on the PLC or gateway exposes tags with names, units and types. SCADA and MES read them securely, from any vendor.
MQTTDevices publish readings to a broker on a topic; dashboards and apps subscribe. Nobody polls; data arrives when it changes.

Good topic names go from big to small: plant, area, machine, signal. Anyone can then subscribe to plant/hsm/# for the whole hot strip mill.

05Predictive maintenance in a rolling mill

A failing bearing vibrates more weeks before it breaks. Watch the trend and change it in a planned shutdown, not in a breakdown.

What it is worth: an unplanned stop of a hot strip mill costs lakhs of rupees an hour in lost production. One avoided breakdown pays for the sensors on the whole line.

06OT cybersecurity: the plant is a target

Connecting the plant brings risk. Attacks on industrial networks have stopped plants and pipelines around the world.

SegmentThe plant network is separated from the office by a firewall and a DMZ. Nothing on the internet reaches a PLC directly.
No unknown USBUSB sticks have carried attacks into plants. Scan them, or use the approved transfer station.
PasswordsNo default passwords on PLCs, HMIs and routers. Personal logins, not one shared one.
Remote accessVendors connect only through the approved, logged remote-access system, never through a modem or TeamViewer on the HMI.
