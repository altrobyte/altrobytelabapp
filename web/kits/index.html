Questions

Are the parts original and tested?

We test every board and module before it ships: boards are flashed with a test program and sensors are read once. If a part arrives dead, send us a photo or video on WhatsApp within 7 days and we replace it.

How do I pay?

After you order, our team confirms stock and the delivery charge on WhatsApp and sends a Cashfree payment link (UPI, cards, net banking). Your kit ships after payment.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 4 to 7 days across India after payment. We share the tracking number on WhatsApp.

I don't know what I need. Can you help?

Yes. Write what you want to build in the order form, or message us on WhatsApp, and an engineer suggests the parts. Every part also has free lessons on our roadmap.

Do you make kits for colleges and labs?

Yes, in bulk for colleges, schools and company labs, with training. Write the number of kits in the form or message us.
