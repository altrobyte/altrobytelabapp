Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know electronics already?

No. The Beginner level starts from what a voltage is. Altron explains in simple Hindi or English and checks you understood before moving on.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The first five modules are free with Altron, and you can open everything free for a week when you sign in with WhatsApp.

Do I need hardware?

Many lessons have simulators in the page. For the products you build, a lab kit is available from ₹4,000, or bring your own ESP32.

Online or in person?

Both. Learn online with Altron and live classes, or join the lab in Indore.
