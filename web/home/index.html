Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know electronics already?

No. The Beginner level starts from what a voltage is. Classes are in simple Hindi or English, and you can ask a doubt any time.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The weekend demo class is free and live, and 4 lessons are free when you sign in with your WhatsApp number.

Do I need hardware?

The plans include a mini lab set-up kit for the products you build. Many lessons also have simulators in the page.

Online or in person?

Both. Join live classes online, or at our lab in Gravity Mall, Indore. Every class is recorded.
