QUESTIONS

Before you start

What do I need?

A laptop with Chrome and a microphone. For the board sessions, an ESP32 (or ESP32-S3) and a USB data cable; the button session needs a push button, and the light-sensor and MQTT sessions an LDR and a 10 kΩ resistor. The Git session needs only your laptop.

Is it free?

You can start every session free, without signing in. After a few minutes Altron asks you to sign in with your WhatsApp number to carry on.

Does the AI see my computer?

No. You choose what to paste into the page, and the page checks it. It never reads your files. If you paste a private SSH key by mistake, the page deletes it at once and warns you.

Hindi or English?

Altron speaks Hinglish by default and can switch to English. The notes on each slide are in simple English, so you can read along.
