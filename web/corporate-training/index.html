Training tracks

Pick a track, or let us combine them for your product

IoT product development

From sensor to cloud dashboard, the way a product ships.

  • ESP32 and ESP32-S3, sensors, power
  • MQTT, HTTP and WebSockets, secure TLS
  • AWS IoT Core and cloud dashboards
  • Over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates
🧠

Edge AI

Intelligence on the device, without a server in the loop.

  • TinyML on ESP32-S3 and STM32
  • Voice and vision at the edge
  • Data collection, training, deployment
  • Anomaly detection for machines
⚙️

Embedded systems & RTOS

Firmware your team can maintain and trust.

  • Embedded C, drivers, interrupts, DMA
  • FreeRTOS: tasks, queues, timing
  • STM32 with HAL and ESP-IDF
  • Debugging with a logic analyser

FAQ

Questions teams ask

Do you train onsite at our office or plant?

Yes. We train onsite anywhere in India, online live, or at our lab in Indore. Onsite training brings the hardware for every participant.

Can the training be built around our own product?

Yes. After a discovery call we shape the labs around your devices, protocols and use cases, for example vehicle telematics or machine monitoring.

What does corporate training cost?

It is priced per trainer-hour (from ₹4,000 an hour) or as a fixed price for a programme, depending on the length, the batch size and the hardware. Request a proposal for an exact quote.

Do participants get a certificate?

Yes. Each participant who completes the assessment gets a certificate that can be checked online at altrobytelab.com/verify.

Do you also set up labs for colleges and institutes?

Yes: a full embedded and IoT lab with curriculum and trainer support. See institute labs.
