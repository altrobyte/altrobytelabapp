IoT & Embedded Systems Intern

2 positionsStipend ₹5,000 – 7,000 / monthWork from office · Indore

What you will do

  • Build and test small ESP32 and IoT projects for our lab, demos and courses
  • Use AI agents (Claude Code, Copilot, ChatGPT) to write, debug and document code faster, and check that what they write really works on the board
  • Keep the lab running: set up boards, sensors and lab kits, test new hardware, and keep everything working for the classes
  • Help learners in the lab and in live classes with wiring, uploading code and small doubts
  • Record short demo videos and write simple notes of what you built

You should know

  • Embedded C: GPIO, timers, interrupts, and reading sensors over ADC, I2C or UART
  • At least one complete project on ESP32, STM32 or Arduino that you can show (GitHub or a video)
  • How Wi-Fi and MQTT or HTTP move data from a board to a server or dashboard
  • Reading a datasheet and a schematic, and debugging with a multimeter
  • Using AI coding tools well: you can tell when the AI's code is wrong and fix it
  • Good to have: FreeRTOS, ESP-IDF, Python, Git

Benefits

  • Monthly stipend of ₹5,000 – 7,000
  • Work on real industrial IoT products, not dummy projects
  • Mentorship from engineers who ship products for industry
  • Full access to the Altrobyte Lab courses and Altron, the AI teacher
  • Our embedded and IoT lab, boards and tools at the office
  • Internship certificate
