Before you join

Are the notes really free?

Yes. Every note on the roadmap is free to read, with a short summary. Class recordings unlock with a paid plan.

What does a live plan cost?

It depends on the plan, the batch and whether you join online or at our Indore centre. Message us on WhatsApp and we send the exact fee and the next start date. EMI is available.

What is the ₹999 seat booking?

It holds your seat in the next live batch, and it counts towards your fee.

Can I see a class before I pay?

Yes. Join a free live demo class from the batches above, and try Altron on any lesson.

Is GST included?

Self-paced is ₹4,999 + 18% GST a month. We send a GST invoice for every payment.
