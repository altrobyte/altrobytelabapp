What you will build

Four real products, not four certificates.

One product grows through the program. Each stage ends with something you can hold, run and show in an interview.

Stage 1 · Month 1

A smart device on your own PCB

An ESP32 product with sensors, relay, servo and an OLED screen, running FreeRTOS, controlled from your phone, and its PCB designed by you in KiCad.

ESP32Embedded CFreeRTOSWi-FiKiCad PCBGit
Stage 2 · Month 2

The product online, with your dashboard

Your soldered board in an enclosure, sending data over MQTT to AWS IoT Core, a Python dashboard with APIs and a database, and safe over-the-air updates.

SolderingMQTTAWS IoT CorePythonREST APIsOTA
Stage 3 · Month 3

Industrial control on STM32

A solar tracker and an industrial dashboard on STM32, speaking CAN and Modbus like real machines, debugged with GDB and tested automatically.

STM32Cortex-M4CANModbusGDBCI tests
Stage 4

A system that sees and predicts

Defect detection on a production line and early fault warnings from sensor data, connected to your IoT system, then a finished portfolio and interview practice.

OpenCVYOLOAI on IoT dataIoT securityPortfolioMock interviews
